### 2.3. Petition Opposing Geldings in the Arc

**Facts:**
A Racing Post headline reports that "More big names sign petition opposing geldings in the Arc - as Teddy Grimthorpe warns of threat to European breeding" (ID 49 | Racing Post Staff | racing_post). While related to breeding, the specific focus on a petition against geldings running in the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe is a niche, yet highly significant, debate within European racing. The Arc has traditionally been a race for entire colts and fillies, serving as a crucial test for future breeding stock. Allowing geldings would fundamentally alter its character and, as warned, could pose a "threat to European breeding" by diminishing the race's role in identifying top-tier stallion and broodmare prospects. This highlights a deep-seated cultural and economic tension within the sport's elite.
