### 1.1 UK Betting Industry Challenges & Regulation

#### Facts
The UK racing industry is facing significant headwinds due to proposed tax increases and stricter regulations on betting. Industry leaders and bookmakers are issuing stark warnings about the potential for widespread betting shop closures, job losses, and the creation of "ghost towns" on high streets. Concerns are also being raised about the impact on the sport itself, with some suggesting that the agenda extends beyond problem gambling to an attempt to eliminate gambling entirely. The landscape is also seeing consolidation, with betting exchanges acquiring competitors.
