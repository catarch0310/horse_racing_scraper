### 1.3. UK Gambling Regulation & Affordability Checks

**Facts:**
The British racing and betting industries are facing significant pressure from proposed government gambling reforms, particularly regarding affordability checks. Entain's boss has warned that potential impacts on betting shops could threaten racing's "ecosystem," highlighting that their retail estate alone contributes £50 million to horseracing. The BHA is actively seeking urgent talks with the new gambling minister, Vicky Foxcroft, on this issue. There's a growing concern that the Gambling Commission may target specific demographics, such as men aged 55 or older, and a strong sentiment that the Commission should not have "free rein" over affordability checks, given its past record of "burying inconvenient evidence." This comes at a critical juncture for British racing, with its funding model already under strain, as evidenced by the Jockey Club's departure from the RCA.
